Introduction: What Is This Page About?

In English, thousands of words are built from smaller, meaningful pieces! A Prefix is a group of letters added to the beginning of a root word or verb to change its meaning (for example, adding re- to write creates rewrite, meaning "to write again").

A Suffix is added to the end of a root word or verb to change its grammatical role or meaning (for example, adding -able to read creates readable, turning a verb into an adjective).
